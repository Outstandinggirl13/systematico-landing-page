Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

At Systematico, we believe that your time and your schedule are inherently private. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent approach to data collection, usage, and protection when you use the Systematico application and website. Our core philosophy is simple: your data belongs to you.

2. Data Collection & Local Storage

Systematico is designed with a "local-first" architecture. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect, transmit, or store your personal tasks, schedules, or habits on our servers.

  • Your Schedules: All data entered into the 24-hour visual dial is stored entirely locally on your personal device.
  • Analytics: We do not use invasive third-party tracking scripts within the app to monitor your daily planning habits.

3. Third-Party Services

Because Systematico is distributed through the Google Play Store, Google may collect basic, anonymized telemetry data (such as crash reports or app download statistics) according to their own privacy policies. This data helps us ensure the app runs smoothly but does not contain your personal schedule data.

  • Google Privacy Policy

4. Data Security

Because your data remains on your device, the security of your Systematico data is directly tied to the security of your hardware. We highly recommend protecting your device with a secure PIN, password, or biometric authentication to prevent unauthorized access to your schedules.

5. Children’s Privacy

Systematico does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information outside of the local app storage, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app's features or legal requirements. We will notify users of any significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
