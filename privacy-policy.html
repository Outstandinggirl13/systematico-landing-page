Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Systematico is designed with privacy in mind.

Data Storage

All schedules, tasks, and settings are stored locally on your device. Systematico does not transmit, collect, or store your planning data on external servers.

Personal Information

Systematico does not require account creation and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Third-Party Services

Systematico is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information according to its own policies and terms. For more information, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

Data Security

Because your data is stored locally on your device, protecting access to your device helps protect your Systematico data.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above.
